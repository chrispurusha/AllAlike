BEFORE YOU UPDATE

Sn2Alike, coming from 1.0.9 or earlier: many of its parameters have moved, so a project saved with the old version
opens with the wrong settings. In each such project:

    1. If you have edited a sound and want to keep it, save it with File > Save Patch As... BEFORE updating.
       The new version cannot recover it from the project.
    2. After updating, load the preset (or the saved patch) again in each Sn2Alike.
    3. Set the plug-in's own settings again too: Voices, Master, Maths, Link, Channel and SysEx ID.
    4. Check any automation and any controllers mapped to Sn2Alike's dials: they may now turn something else.
    5. Save the project.

Your preset library (Presets.syx) is not affected. Projects using the other plug-ins should open as before.
Keep a copy of a project you care about before opening it in a new version: these are betas, and later ones may move
parameters again.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Pulse2Alike, TiAlike, Sn2Alike and Nl3Alike are betas. UltraAlike is new here and an alpha: it plays banks (.E4B, .exb),
SFZ and loose samples, but has no filters, LFOs or modulation yet, and its saved projects may not survive an update.
OneShot and ModAlike are usable but work in progress. ProphAlike has its first filter and little else.

Sn2Alike sounds different from 1.0.9, and closer to the instrument: the EQ, Distortion and dry level now follow
measurements of it, so most sounds with effects on have more bass. FLOAT (the default) is the fast one; FIXED
follows the instrument's own arithmetic and costs more. They agree closely on most sounds, not on all. Not built yet:
the arpeggiator (programs that use it play as plain notes), the Phaser (it plays as a chorus), the comb, Performances,
and the audio inputs. Its chorus, delay and reverb levels are still approximate.

Nl3Alike: the LFOs' Clock, Divisor and Mono, Transpose and the arpeggiator's Keyboard Sync are on the
panel but do nothing yet, and the LFOs' Smooth and Special waves play as Random and Triangle.

In every plug-in, holding Shift while dragging a dial moves it a quarter as far. Sn2Alike, Nl3Alike and TiAlike
show their dials in units (dB, ms, Hz, semitones) where that is known, and the plain number where it is not.

Known problems and everything missing: Docs/feature-status.md at https://github.com/chrispurusha/AllAlike
